FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Find answers to common questions about Veryfy, verification and reporting suspicious products.

How does Veryfy work?

Veryfy combines inspection of a physical HiddenMark with a live lookup of a product’s Uni-Code and available product records.

How can I report a suspected counterfeit product?

Use the reporting channel when available, and keep product details, photographs and purchase information where safe.

How do I know if a product is genuine?

Inspect the label, verify its code through the official service, and read the full result and any warnings. A matching code alone cannot prove that the physical item is genuine.

Is product verification free?

Any future service costs or mobile-network charges will be communicated by the service operator.

What if a product isn’t found in the database?

Do not treat an unrecognized result as proof of authenticity. Double-check the code and follow the official guidance.

Where does Veryfy’s information come from?

The planned platform uses participating brand records, verification signals and relevant safety reports.
